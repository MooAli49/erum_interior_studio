## Header

Keep the main navigation intentionally simple.

Suggested:

-   Projects
-   Services
-   About
-   Knowledge
-   Contact
-   Language Switcher
-   Start Your Project CTA

Process, Meet The Engineer, and detailed content categories do not need
to occupy the main navigation.

## Footer

Suggested groups:

-   Short About
-   Navigation
-   Services
-   Contact
-   Social Media
-   WhatsApp
-   Privacy Policy
-   Terms

## Content Relationship Model

The website should create a connected content graph:

``` text
Content
  ↓
Project
  ↓
Service
  ↓
Lead CTA
```

A visitor may enter through an article or video and eventually reach a
related project, service, and inquiry form.
