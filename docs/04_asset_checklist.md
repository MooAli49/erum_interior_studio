## Asset Quality Guidelines

Whenever possible:

-   Send original files.
-   Avoid screenshots.
-   Avoid compressed WhatsApp images.
-   Keep project assets organized by project.
-   Keep renders, construction, final, and videos separated.

Suggested structure:

``` text
Projects/
├── Project-01/
│   ├── Render/
│   ├── Construction/
│   ├── Final/
│   ├── Before-After/
│   ├── Floor-Plans/
│   └── Videos/
└── Project-02/
```
